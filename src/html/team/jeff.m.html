Jeff Mullins

UX Designer

His passion lies in devising creative solutions to large-scale, intricate problems, ensuring intuitive and impactful user experiences.

Outside the office, he is an avid adventurer, embracing the call of the wild through hiking, biking, and boating. He is also a dedicated parent, trying to keep pace with the latest Roblox trends alongside his son, which fuels his creativity and keeps him connected with the pulse of next-gen user engagement.
