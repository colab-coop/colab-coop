John is a user experience designer, trombonist and community-oriented human keen on leveraging human-centered design principles where they can best augment human wellbeing, such as in our own communities, not-for-profit organizations and collectively-owned organizations.

He has worked in university, Fortune 150, non-profit contexts and more, using technologies like Figma and Miro to craft sensible, robust digital experiences and products that empathically align with user needs and desires.

Pursuing a career in human-centered design, John first became enthralled with the cooperative movement during his undergraduate studies, as a member of the Inter-Cooperative Council at the University of Michigan.

John is big on cooking large group dinners for and with his friends, a love he inherited from his mother. Other than that, he spends his time outdoors, in the depths of personal projects, or learning about the world and its people.
