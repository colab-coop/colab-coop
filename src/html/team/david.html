David resides in what is colonially called Alabama, on unceded Muskogee-Creek ancestral land and is enthusiastic about ending the fossil fuel and war-driven imperialist oligarchy and ushering in a new age of living in harmony with nature and putting people above profit. He participates in several community organizing / solidarity / mutual aid efforts to prepare for such a change. David is also very interested in using Sociocracy for self-governance in organizations and perhaps one day in the public sphere. He is currently enjoying programming in Elixir and can't wait to see a Solarpunk future. David hopes you live long and prosper.
