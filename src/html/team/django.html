At CoLab, Django is a developer for WordPress sites. His approach is people-centric, which influences the usability, performance and accessibility of his designs.

Django earned a Bachelor of Arts in sociology and anthropology from Concordia University, which he feels has relevance to his work, which lies at the intersection of people and technology. This work continues with his contributions to the WordPress ActivityPub plugin. He was initially drawn to technology by its power to democratize media.

Django has a special interest in language revitalization. He is currently studying Irish, as well as Cree, one of the largest language groups among First Nations in Canada.

He also enjoys hiking, exploring and food. He’ll try any kind of food, especially if it’s spicy.

Django is a dual Canadian and Irish citizen.
