Colleen joined CoLab in 2014 and became a member owner in 2016. She serves as People Circle Lead, Project Manager, and member of the Business Development Circle, and regularly contributes to the CoLab Board.

Colleen believes in a regenerative economy, where business can drive social, economic, and climate healing. She is passionate about democratic workplaces and advocates for the global cooperative movement. Colleen's personal mission is to make workplaces better for workers, starting with CoLab.

In her leadership capacity, she brings expertise in collaboration dynamics and organizational development, acting as facilitator, mediator, and coach while helping shape policy.

Colleen is project manager trained in agile processes. Through cultivating strong team dynamics and clear processes, she helps create the conditions for teams to do their best work and serve clients with depth and excellence.
