Peter Hebert is a web developer working with Drupal and WordPress, PHP and front-end technologies and frameworks. Peter contributes to client projects in both strategic and technical roles, and is an advocate for a better, more inclusive user experience for website users.

Peter has a wide range of experience in the technology and arts sectors, working with various web and graphic design agencies, the Canadian federal public service, the Vancouver 2010 Olympics, the Vancouver International Film festival, as well as being a freelancer and entrepreneur.

Peter is actively involved with the open-source software community - he has been a presenter at WordCamps and various tech meetups, and has helped organize WordCamps in both Vancouver and Montreal.

Peter also has had a lifelong interest in the arts. He has produced and directed several short films, was involved in the independent filmmaker communities in both Ottawa and Vancouver, and has volunteered with many cultural festivals across Canada.

In his free time, Peter enjoys cooking, movies, hi-fi, traveling with his wife, Hélène, and hiking in the forest with their loveable Bernese Mountain Dog.
