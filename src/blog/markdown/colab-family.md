# Welcome to the CoLab Family

CoLab clients are the best. No, seriously, our clients are amazing; otherwise they wouldn't be our clients! Our existing clients already know, but potential future clients should be made aware: we are here working with you because we want to be here working with you. The team you'll be working with are not just-here-for-a-paycheck employees, but actual owners of the company. We believe in you and your vision, and we want to make it a reality. We think you are making the world a better place, and so we are passionate about what you are bringing to life!

When you join the CoLab family, it means we (the worker owners) believe what you're doing is great, and stand with you to support you in whatever way we can. You aren't just hiring a consultant, you're gaining a comrade. Business-as-usual corporate entities care about one thing and one thing only: profit. But not CoLab; we *actually want* to see your project succeed. Of course we all exist within the oppressive context of capitalism and so we do need to charge for our services, but we don't just disappear when the project is done. We're here for you, we'll check in on you. We want to make sure your good work is continuing!

CoLab clients are more than just a job for us; our clients form a real human collective, with real human relationships. This also means that we might be able to help you with networking! Our clients range far and wide, and you may find that we know JUST the right person / group for you to get in touch with. All our clients are aligned with the idea of making the world a better place, so you'll be in good company, literally!

Joining the CoLab family means you're hiring not just a collaborator but a cheerleader, a fan, a friend. Some of the relationships we build while in contract extend well beyond the work context. Some of our worker-owners have come from within the ranks of our clients, and vice-versa! When we say you're entering into a family, we mean it.

We are human-centered, not profit-centered. That's what it means to be a part of the CoLab family.
