# CoLab Collaborates

## In the worker-owned coop world, it is about cooperation not competition.

Our clients choose CoLab for many reasons, but one of our best-known strengths is collaboration (we even named ourselves in honor of it!). In the corporate capitalist world, it is "dog eat dog," as they say. We firmly reject that inhuman mindset. We believe that human beings' natural state is that of cooperation and collaboration. Indeed nothing would be possible if all we did was compete. No. Solidarity, compassion, and collaboration are the best parts of being human, and that is what we embrace.

CoLab does not take the approach of mercenary-for-hire. When a client comes to CoLab, our goal is not to "build a thing and get paid," but rather "co-create visions." We don't set ourselves in a position of authorities that tell you what is best for you, but rather experts that support your goals and work with you to get there. You know what you need better than anyone, and we will work *with* you to figure out exactly what that is, and how to get there. We won't just disappear for a time, then come back and say "here's your order!" No, we will involve you in every step of the way. We'll be showing you what we're building as it is being built. This method of collaborative co-creation ensures that what you envision is what is built.

This cooperative and collaborative philosophy extends beyond our relationships with clients. We use this approach within the cooperative, with one-another. We also take this stance with other cooperatives. We don't consider other cooperatives our competitors but rather collaborators. Indeed, we learn from one-another, support one-another, and even fulfill under-capacity between cooperatives. The entire cooperative sector is a macro cooperative, in a sense. Indeed, Principle 6 of [The Rochdale Principles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochdale_Principles) is "Cooperation Among Cooperatives," which means not only are we, within a cooperative, all in it together, but all worker-owned cooperatives are in it together as a whole.

That said, we DO believe in competition: competition with the status-quo. We believe we are a better choice than business-as-usual, and that we are not a usual business. When you choose CoLab, you choose collaboration, not cut-throatism; you choose human-first, not profit-first.
