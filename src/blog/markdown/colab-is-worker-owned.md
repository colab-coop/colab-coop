# CoLab is Worker-Owned

Do you know what a worker-owned coop is?

When many people think of coops, if they even know they exist, they think of consumer coops like REI or perhaps producer coops like Ocean Spray. But CoLab Cooperative is a worker-owned cooperative, which means not only do the workers own the company, they manage it too. We don't have owners taking profits despite doing nothing, and we don't have non-worker shareholders telling us how to run the business. We are each, individually, our own bosses. We're not a machine; we're a community.

What this translates to, in terms of serving our clients, is this: you're not getting a business owner who hires out the work to employees, you're getting an entire team of business owners. You're not getting a faceless entity with replaceable cogs, but a team of individuals who care about more than just a paycheck.

Orthodox corporate employees without any say in where the fruits of their labor go, nor any say in how the work gets accomplished, has little incentive to do their best work, except society's threat of unemployment and possibly unhealthy internal competition for higher wages.

But a business where every worker can say "this is my company," and mean it? That's a different dynamic. That is a team of people who share a vision and want to see it realized. That is a real work family with shared stakes in a shared resource, and CoLab clients can feel that difference.

This way of working is great for our workers-owners. We operate on consensus-based decision-making, meaning everyone's voice is heard and respected. Our worker-owners are free to be their fullest selves at CoLab, and we give ourselves great flexibility in how that is expressed. For the most part, we choose our own hours, and our own work.

But this way of working is also great for our clients. Some of us have no problem working weekends or holidays because the "work week" already strikes a great work/life balance. Add to that the fact that CoLab consists of international worker-owners, and you have an almost 24/7 team dedicated to bringing your vision to life.

We believe in our clients, and we want them to succeed. Not because we want to get rich, but because we care. If we didn't care, or if all we wanted was to get rich, we would be working somewhere else. And if our clients didn't care about that, they'd look somewhere else too. Because we're a worker-owned cooperative, each worker-owner has a say in how the business is run. And we have decided, collectively, that our clients are going to be the ones that are strictly beneficial for planet Earth and her inhabitants. We won't be taking on clients that destroy the environment, exploit customers and under-represented communities, or support the military industrial complex.

What this means is that every worker-owner at CoLab fully supports our clients, and that we will always put our best effort into the projects we know will make the world a better place.
